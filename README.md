# Pixelito

An 8-bit app for learning Spanish, set in Tuber Falls, Idaho (population 412, potatoes: many more). Works offline and installs to the home screen.

**The story:** you're a gringo who wants to move somewhere warm where they speak Spanish. Your Spanish: "taco." Abuela Chela, a potato farmer and the only Spanish speaker in town, pays you in potatoes to learn. Her grandson Beto (sixteen, very confident, often wrong) is your teacher. 100 potatoes buys a bus ticket south. Beto says the driver accepts potatoes. He does not.

The story plays the first time the app opens (replay it in Ajustes).

## Tabs

- **Hoy**: 16 lessons, one a day, plus **Mis lecciones**.
  - Each lesson: **Frases** (whole phrases, heard and said aloud, checked by the microphone) → **Escena** (a short scene using them) → **¡En vivo!** (a timed live conversation: answer out loud or tap) → potatoes.
  - Half are friendly chats, half are **pleitos**: arguments with Beto, who is calm and absolutely wrong.
  - **Mis lecciones**: tap *+ Crear mi lección*, name it, type 3+ phrases (Spanish, English optional), pick friendly or argument, done. Beto turns them into a lesson.
- **Historias**: short reads from Tuber Falls. Underlined words are tappable: hear them, mark *Ya la sé*, or add them to your cards. *Modo difícil* hides all English.
- **Tarjetas**: swipe flashcards. ← *No sé* · ↑ *Aprendiendo* · *Ya sé* →. Known cards come back later and later (spaced repetition); unknown ones come back soon. Starts with words that are hard to say and false friends; lesson phrases, missed answers and story words land here too. Add your own at the bottom. Flip direction (English → Spanish) for speaking practice.
- **Ajustes**: answer timer, audio-only mode, microphone test, voice, backup/restore.

## Potatoes

Lesson: 3 (5 if perfect) · your own lesson: 2 (4 if perfect) · story: 1 · every 10 flashcards: 1. Goals: 100 a bus ticket, 250 a hammock, 500 a little house on the beach, 1000 a visit from Abuela.

## Putting it on a phone

Repo Settings → Pages → deploy from this branch, open the link on the phone, then "Add to Home Screen". After the first visit it works offline (speech recognition may need a connection).

- Spanish voice on iPhone: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → Spanish.
- Microphone on iPhone: turn on Settings → General → Keyboard → Enable Dictation, and allow the microphone for Safari. Use *Probar micrófono* in Ajustes to check.

Everything is saved on the device. Use *Exportar* in Ajustes to keep a backup. Progress from the earlier "Pixel Español" version carries over automatically.
